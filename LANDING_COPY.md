## FAQ

**Q: Is this really free?**
A: Yes, 100% free. No login required. No credit card. No "premium tier" that unlocks the actual savings. We make money when high-savings users choose to explore discounted AI credits through Credex — but that's optional and never required.

**Q: How accurate are the savings estimates?**
A: Our engine uses current vendor pricing data verified weekly against official pricing pages. Every recommendation traces to a specific plan comparison with a cited source. The savings estimates are directionally accurate — the exact amount depends on your actual usage patterns, which we can't see from pricing data alone.

**Q: Do you store my data?**
A: Only if you choose to save your report by entering your email. Audit results are generated entirely in your browser by default. If you share a report, only tool names and savings numbers are included — never your email, company name, or team details.

**Q: Which AI tools do you support?**
A: We currently support Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf. These cover the vast majority of startup AI spending. We add new tools based on user demand.

**Q: How is this different from just checking pricing pages?**
A: Three ways: (1) We compare your entire stack holistically, catching overlaps between tools. (2) We factor in your team size and use case to recommend the right plan, not just the cheapest one. (3) We surface savings from credit programs (like Credex) that aren't listed on vendor pricing pages.
